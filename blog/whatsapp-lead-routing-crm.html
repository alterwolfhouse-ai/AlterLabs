How to route WhatsApp leads into a CRM

WhatsApp is where many Indian businesses get real enquiries. The problem is that follow-up often stays trapped inside chats.

2026-05-28 / 6 min / whatsapp, crm, lead routing

A WhatsApp lead system should capture the contact, source, enquiry type, owner and next action. Without that structure, high-intent leads can disappear inside busy inboxes.

Workflow map

  • Customer sends WhatsApp enquiry.
  • Team records or syncs lead details into CRM.
  • Lead is tagged by source, product and urgency.
  • Owner and next action are assigned.
  • Dashboard shows pending follow-ups.

AI can help summarize the chat and suggest intent, but the CRM should remain the source of truth for ownership and status.

AI customer support triage with human review

AI can help sort support messages faster, but escalation and final replies need clear ownership.

2026-05-28 / 5 min / ai, support, triage

AI can classify

  • Urgency and sentiment.
  • Billing, delivery, technical or account issue.
  • Existing customer vs new enquiry.
  • Suggested reply draft.
  • Escalation requirement.

Keep an audit trail. Support quality improves when AI reduces sorting time but does not hide accountability.

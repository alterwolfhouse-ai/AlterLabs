AI agents in CRM: risks and controls

AI agents can help with CRM hygiene and follow-up, but only when permissions and review rules are clear.

2026-05-28 / 6 min / ai agents, crm, risk

Risks to control

  • Updating the wrong record.
  • Changing deal stages without human context.
  • Drafting messages from incomplete data.
  • Creating duplicate tasks or noisy reminders.
  • Hiding errors inside automation logs.

Useful controls

Start read-only where possible. Let AI detect missing fields, summarize notes and propose actions. Add write permissions only after audit trails, fallback rules and ownership are working.

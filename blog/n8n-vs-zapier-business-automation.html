n8n vs Zapier for business automation

Both tools can connect forms, CRMs, email and spreadsheets. The right choice depends on control, complexity and maintenance.

2026-05-28 / 6 min / n8n, zapier, automation

Zapier fits when

  • The workflow is simple and common.
  • The team wants quick setup with low technical overhead.
  • Most apps already have strong Zapier connectors.

n8n fits when

  • You need more control over branching and data transformation.
  • Webhooks and custom APIs are involved.
  • You want to self-host or keep complex workflows visible.

The tool matters less than the workflow map. Define trigger, action, owner, error state and fallback before building.

AI workflow automation works best with human review

AI is useful when it drafts, classifies, enriches and flags work. It becomes risky when it silently changes business records without context.

2026-05-28 / 5 min / ai, workflow, governance

Every AI workflow should define what the model can suggest, what it can update, and where a human must approve the action.

Good AI workflow use cases

  • Classify inbound enquiries by intent and urgency.
  • Draft follow-up replies for review.
  • Summarize long CRM notes or call transcripts.
  • Flag missing fields or unusual records.
  • Prepare daily action lists for operators.

Guardrails matter

Use logs, confidence thresholds, fallback rules and clear owners. The goal is not full autonomy on day one. The goal is lower admin with better visibility.

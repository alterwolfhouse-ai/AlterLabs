Operations dashboard for service businesses

A service business dashboard should show what is pending, who owns it and what is blocked.

2026-05-28 / 5 min / operations, dashboard, service

Useful blocks

  • New enquiries and assigned owner.
  • Open jobs by status.
  • Delayed tasks or SLA risks.
  • Revenue expected this week.
  • Customer issues needing attention.

The dashboard should support the daily standup, not replace the people running the work.

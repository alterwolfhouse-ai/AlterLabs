Fix data hygiene before adding AI automation

AI automation is only as useful as the data it can trust. Dirty CRM records turn intelligent workflows into confident confusion.

2026-05-28 / 5 min / crm, data, ai

What to clean first

  • Duplicate contacts and companies.
  • Missing phone, email, source and owner fields.
  • Undefined lifecycle stages.
  • Old deals with no next action.
  • Inconsistent product or service names.

Once the fields are clean, AI can classify, summarize and flag exceptions more reliably. Without data hygiene, automation mostly moves mess faster.

Simple rule

If a human cannot explain what a field means, an AI workflow should not be trusted to update it.

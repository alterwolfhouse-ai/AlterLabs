Quick answer

AlterLabs prices the E-Commerce Starter Pack at ₹14,999 for product listings up to 20 products, cart / WhatsApp order flow, payment gateway guidance, mobile-friendly pages and basic SEO + analytics.

Use this when you need a real catalog and order path, but do not yet need a heavy marketplace-style build.

The right package is not automatically the cheapest or the largest. It is the smallest defined scope that can create the business outcome without hiding essential work in later add-ons.

Who it suits

Business owners comparing website, content, advertising or maintenance budgets before committing to a build.

What to expect

A defined deliverable, delivery window and lead path rather than an open-ended promise to do everything.

Best next step

Share the exact offer, deadline and budget range so the scope can be confirmed before work begins.

What this budget should help you publish or improve

A small package should still create a useful customer-facing outcome. For this offer, prioritize the following work instead of spreading the budget across unrelated extras:

  • Write product category pages with buying criteria.
  • Create product description templates for every SKU.
  • Publish one guide that answers delivery, warranty, customization and payment questions.

These are not filler deliverables. Each item should answer a buyer question, support a campaign or make the next customer action clearer. If a proposed package cannot explain that connection, ask for a tighter scope.

How to choose the right package

If you need one focused campaign surface, start with the ₹2,999 landing page. If you need a first business website with domain and hosting, use the ₹3,500 starter website. If the business needs multiple service pages and a lead flow, choose the ₹7,500 business website. If the website already exists and needs small changes, use the ₹900 update or ₹5,000/year maintenance plan.

For traffic and content, pair the page with Meta Ads Setup at ₹2,999, Google Search Ads at ₹3,999, Social Media Creatives from ₹999 onwards and Content Generation from ₹1,999 onwards.

Step 01

Choose one outcome

Decide whether the immediate goal is credibility, campaign leads, product enquiries, content production or keeping an existing site current.

Step 02

Confirm the boundary

Write down page count, content inputs, revisions, integrations, delivery date and anything specifically excluded from the listed price.

Step 03

Connect the lead path

Confirm what happens after a click, form or WhatsApp message. A cheap page that loses enquiries is not a useful saving.

What to confirm before you pay

✓The exact pages, sections or deliverables
✓Who supplies copy, images, products and approvals
✓Domain, hosting, advertising spend and third-party fees
✓Revision limits, delivery timing and post-launch support
✓How enquiries are captured and handed to the team
✓What would require a larger custom scope

A written boundary protects both sides. It prevents a low entry price from turning into surprise exclusions, and it prevents a focused package from being judged against work it was never designed to include.

Questions buyers usually ask

Is the listed price the complete project cost?

It is the complete AlterLabs price for the listed entry scope. Advertising spend, paid software, payment-gateway charges, domain renewals or work outside that scope may be separate and should be confirmed before the project starts.

Can the package be customized?

Yes, but additions can change price and delivery time. The most useful approach is to keep the first release focused, launch it and add only what real buyer behaviour shows is needed.

What information should I send for an accurate answer?

Share your business, main offer, target location, preferred package, deadline and examples of any existing website or content. That is enough to confirm fit or recommend a better starting point.
